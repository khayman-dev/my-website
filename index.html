About me

I turn new technology into products people actually use.

I'm a product and people leader with more than twenty years of experience across AI software, connected hardware, industrial IoT, and building controls. I've taken products from customer research through launch on embedded devices, desktop, mobile, web, and the cloud, and I've hired, coached, and developed the teams that delivered them.

Most recently, at Phaidra, I led a nine-person team that brought an equipment anomaly and fault detection product to market. It runs on live data from connected equipment, and operators use it through an LLM interface. We shipped the general release in four months. It caught faults that existing control systems missed, and it grew from a single-site beta into a multi-year, twelve-site enterprise contract.

Before that, I owned a $20M portfolio of building controllers at Honeywell, launched industrial cellular routers at Digi International, and helped equipment makers bring connected products to market at Exosite. I spent fourteen years at Trane, where I brought the company's first mobile app for building automation to market (and was named on five U.S. patents for it) and led a team of business analysts and UX architects for a decade.

I trained as a mechanical engineer at Queen's University and later earned a master's in Organizational Leadership from St. Catherine University. My thesis research looked at how women can find sponsorship and advance into leadership in corporations.
